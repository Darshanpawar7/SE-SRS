# Delivery signals

Six selected-sprint metrics show WIP (In Progress + Review), review queue, unfinished critical items, unfinished items without a recognised owner, work not updated for at least seven elapsed days, and elapsed UTC calendar days. Priority mix reports completed / total item counts. The attention queue opens affected tasks directly and explains each rule. Counts are derived live from existing data; zero/unknown states are explicit. A definitions panel documents all formulas.

This does not infer blockers, cycle time, throughput, forecast delivery or a health score. Future/unknown update timestamps are excluded from stale counts and reported separately. Calendar completion is separate from work completion. No schema migration is required.
